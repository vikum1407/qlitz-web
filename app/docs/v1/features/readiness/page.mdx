# Readiness

The Readiness tab gives you an objective, calculated score for whether your project is ready to release. Instead of a manual pre-release checklist, Readiness aggregates coverage, test results, and risk signals into a single score with a clear breakdown.

## The readiness score

The readiness score is a number from 0 to 100, calculated from four weighted components:

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Component</th>
      <th>Weight</th>
      <th>Description</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Requirement Coverage</strong></td>
      <td>40%</td>
      <td>Percentage of in-scope requirements with passing linked tests</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Pass Rate</strong></td>
      <td>30%</td>
      <td>Percentage of test cases passing in the most recent run</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Critical Requirement Coverage</strong></td>
      <td>20%</td>
      <td>Coverage of requirements marked as Critical priority specifically</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td><strong>Flakiness Penalty</strong></td>
      <td>10%</td>
      <td>Deduction based on the proportion of flaky tests in the suite</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

A score of 80 or above is the default threshold for **Ready to Ship** status. This threshold can be adjusted in project settings.

## Readiness levels

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Score</th>
      <th>Level</th>
      <th>Meaning</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>90–100</td>
      <td><strong>Ship-ready</strong></td>
      <td>All critical requirements covered, high pass rate, low flakiness</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>80–89</td>
      <td><strong>Ready with caveats</strong></td>
      <td>Minor gaps exist; risk is understood and accepted</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>60–79</td>
      <td><strong>At risk</strong></td>
      <td>Significant coverage gaps or failure clusters present</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Below 60</td>
      <td><strong>Not ready</strong></td>
      <td>Critical requirements uncovered or pass rate too low</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

## Scoped readiness

Project-level readiness covers all requirements. When you create a release, readiness is automatically scoped to the requirements linked to that release window. This means you can assess readiness for a specific sprint or milestone without the noise of unrelated requirements.

## Readiness over time

The Readiness tab shows a trend chart of your readiness score across the last 10 runs. A consistently improving trend indicates a healthy QA process. A sudden drop is a signal to investigate before proceeding with a release.

## Readiness and the release state machine

Readiness drives the release status machine. When you run an assessment on a release:

1. Qlitz calculates the readiness score for that release's scoped requirements
2. If the score meets the threshold, the release automatically advances to **Ready to Ship**
3. If the score does not meet the threshold, the release stays in **Validating** and the breakdown shows exactly which components are falling short
