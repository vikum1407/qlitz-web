# AI Quality Engine

The **AI Quality Engine** is the intelligence layer of Qlitz.  
It continuously analyzes your systems, changes, and test signals to surface **real quality risk** before it impacts users.

---

## Why it matters

Most teams either:

- Over‑test the wrong things, or  
- Under‑test the critical paths  

The AI Quality Engine helps you focus on what actually matters by:

- Highlighting risky areas of the system  
- Suggesting where to test deeper  
- Reducing noise from low‑value checks  

---

## What it does

### Automated risk detection

Qlitz ingests signals from:

- Code changes  
- Test runs  
- Production behavior  
- Historical incidents  

It then identifies **high‑risk areas** and surfaces them as prioritized quality concerns.

---

### Intelligent test generation

Based on risk and behavior, Qlitz can:

- Propose new test scenarios  
- Suggest missing edge cases  
- Highlight gaps in coverage  

The goal is not to generate random tests, but to **generate meaningful tests** aligned with real risk.

---

### Predictive quality scoring

Qlitz computes a **quality score** for:

- Features  
- Services  
- Releases  

This score reflects:

- Historical stability  
- Current risk signals  
- Test coverage and effectiveness  

Teams use this to decide:

- “Are we ready to ship?”  
- “Where should we invest next in quality?”
