# Enterprise Reliability

The **Enterprise Reliability** layer is what makes Qlitz suitable for serious, large‑scale engineering organizations.

It’s designed for teams that care about **security, compliance, performance, and integration** with existing ecosystems.

---

## Why it matters

As teams scale, quality tooling must:

- Handle large workloads  
- Integrate with complex systems  
- Respect security and compliance boundaries  

Qlitz is built with this in mind from day one.

---

## What it does

### SOC‑ready architecture

Qlitz is designed to align with enterprise security and compliance expectations, including:

- Clear data boundaries  
- Auditable behavior  
- Configurable access controls  

This makes it easier to bring Qlitz into regulated or security‑sensitive environments.

---

### Scalable distributed execution

Qlitz can:

- Distribute workloads across multiple workers  
- Scale horizontally with demand  
- Keep execution times predictable as your test suite grows  

This ensures quality doesn’t become a bottleneck as your system and team scale.

---

### Deep CI/CD integrations

Qlitz integrates with:

- CI providers  
- Deployment pipelines  
- Notification and incident tools  

This allows you to:

- Block or gate releases based on quality signals  
- Surface quality insights where engineers already work  
- Treat quality as a **first‑class signal** in your delivery process
