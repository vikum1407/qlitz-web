# What is Qlitz?

Qlitz is an AI‑powered quality platform for modern engineering teams that want to ship fast **without** sacrificing reliability.

Instead of treating quality as a slow, manual gate at the end of delivery, Qlitz turns it into an **intelligent, continuous system** that runs alongside development, CI/CD, and production.

---

## Why Qlitz exists

Traditional QA struggles with:

- Slow, manual test creation and maintenance  
- Flaky test suites that erode trust  
- Limited visibility into real risk  
- Quality checks that happen too late  

Qlitz was built to solve these problems by combining:

- **AI‑driven analysis** of systems, changes, and behavior  
- **End‑to‑end automation** of the quality lifecycle  
- **Enterprise‑grade reliability** for serious engineering teams  

---

## How Qlitz fits into your stack

Qlitz integrates with:

- Your CI/CD pipelines  
- Your environments and infrastructure  
- Your existing test suites and tools  

It doesn’t replace your engineering team — it **amplifies** it, by making quality:

- More intelligent  
- More automated  
- More predictable  
- More aligned with how you actually ship software
